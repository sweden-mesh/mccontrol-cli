History

Every command sent to a device is kept with what came back: the time, the
command, the topic, the result code in words and the reply. A device's
history is shown newest first, and any line opens the whole reply.

Whether the node signed the reply is kept too: a reply whose signature
failed is shown as "BAD SIGNATURE <reply>" and teaches nothing; one from
firmware that does not sign (older than ar-11) is shown "(unsigned)".

A command sent through a relay is kept with the relay, the route, how it
was sent and how well the relay heard the answer.

A send to a region is kept once per device, each with its own line, and
once for the region with how many answered.

The files are under your configuration directory, history/<node>.jsonl,
one line of JSON per command. See [[cli]] for "mccontrol history".
