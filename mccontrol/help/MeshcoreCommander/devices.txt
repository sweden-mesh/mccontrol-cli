Devices

A device is a repeater you manage: a name you choose, its node id (the
first eight hex characters of its public key, which is how the firmware
names it on the control topics), for lou-19 and older the region its
observers use (the IATA code, such as JKG), and notes.

F4 edits the cursor device. F8 forgets it after a confirmation; its
history file stays on disk. Devices are added by [[discover]] or by hand
on the command line (see [[cli]]).

A node on lou-20 or later needs no region: it is reached by its whole
key alone, wherever it is. A device on lou-19 or older is reached
through its region; with none it cannot be sent to, so give it one
with F4. A region belongs to the device, not to the program.

Names and versions from the fleet. "n" (Devices > Names and versions)
sends "get name" and "ver" to the tagged devices, or the cursor one: a
device you have not named takes the name the node answers with, a name
you gave is kept, and every device stores its firmware version, shown
short in the Ver column (ar-10 1.17.1) and sortable. Two signed reads
per device, each one of the node's command slots this hour; more than
three devices asks first. Firmware that admits neither read answers
not-allowed and the device keeps what it had. "mccontrol refresh" does
the same from the command line.

The whole key. A node on the firmware of 2026-10-08 or later (ar-11)
listens on a topic named by its whole 64-character key; older firmware
by the first 8. The tool keeps the whole key on the device when it
knows it -- a discovery sees it on the node's answer, and "mccontrol add
<name> <64 hex>" stores it -- and addresses each node by the
form its firmware uses. The 8-character id stays the name you see.
