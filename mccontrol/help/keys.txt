Keys

Each interface has keys of its own, and its help lists them under this
topic: "mccontrol --ui <name>", then its help. Plain "mccontrol" starts
the last one asked for, or Monolith when none has been or the one kept
is gone. Every verb is also on the command line, one subcommand each;
see [[cli]].

Discovery asks for no region: it reads the alive messages of your
lou-20 and later nodes, wherever they are, and each interface's help
names the key that starts it. Only lou-19 and older nodes are found by
region, through a discovery of their own ("discover <region>" on the
command line). See [[discover]].
